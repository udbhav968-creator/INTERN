4.2 Difficulty: what the accuracy really measures

Formula labels agree with human-verified labels at accuracy 41.9%, Cohen's kappa 0.07. The old random forest's 77.9% only measures how well it re-learns that formula. v2.1, trained on human labels, scores 82.2% (macro-F1 0.769, ECE 0.0152) with paper-grouped validation.

4.3 Chapter-wise reality check

17 of 54 reliably measured chapters do not beat guessing the majority tier; the headline accuracy is partly carried by the "medium" majority. Only 104 of 411 topics have enough held-out rows to judge.

6. New feature: post-attempt AIR estimate

After an attempt the student gets an indicative All-India Rank range, built from mock difficulty, chapter-wise performance, previous-year chapter trends and real NEET facts. API: GET /students/{id}/air_estimate?paper_number=N&syllabus_mode=all|nmc_reduced. Code: model/air_estimator.py.

6.1 Parameters and how each is used

ParameterHow it enters
Mock difficultyIRT 3PL equating: item parameters (a, b, c) of the questions answered convert raw marks to NEET-scale marks; the report states whether the paper was easier or harder than the reference form.
Chapter-wise performanceChapter mastery from IRT + BKT with hierarchical shrinkage; chapters never attempted are marked prior_only.
Previous-year trendsTaxonomy weightage 2013-2026: mean share, last-3-year share, rising/falling/stable; expected questions per paper = 180 x share (renormalised for the syllabus mode).
Chapter difficultyMean IRT b of the chapter PYQs, advanced-tier share, mean ideal time.
Real NEET factsSourced candidates appeared and general cut-off for 2024-2026; the same marks are re-read on the curve shifted by the cut-off difference (an approximation).
Where to focusmarks at stake = expected questions x 5 x (target mastery - mastery); AIR places gained if only that chapter reached the target (not additive).

Equating makes NEET-style forms interchangeable: the three forms give equated marks within 8 marks of each other (24 simulated students each, so sampling noise included). On deliberately extreme papers the raw gap of 740 marks shrinks to 142 equated marks but does not vanish, and the AIR range widens (median width 1,654,413 on the hard paper, 392,829 on the easy one, versus 206,289 on a NEET-style form). A paper far too hard or too easy carries little information; the report exposes both facts instead of hiding them.
